Privacy policy

Hermes is a private assistant used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as email, calendar, files and health data) only to help the owner, and only after the owner signs in and grants access.

The data is stored on the owner's own computer. To answer the owner's requests, parts of it are sent to AI model providers that the owner has chosen. It is not sold, shared with other people, or used for advertising.

The owner can remove Hermes' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
